cycli.cal — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 11 October 2026

Summary
cycli.cal does not collect your data. Everything you log stays on your
iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What we collect
Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no information from the app.

The app stores what you enter — the days you log and your cycle length
settings — in a file inside the app's own storage on your device. This
file is never transmitted. We cannot read it, and neither can anyone else
without access to your unlocked phone.

Third parties
None. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting
and no third-party software development kits of any kind. No data is
shared with anyone, including any AI service.

Storage and security
Your data is written with iOS file protection, which encrypts it while
your device is locked. It is deliberately excluded from iCloud and from
device backups, so your reproductive health information is not copied to
any cloud account.

Because of this, uninstalling the app, resetting your phone or losing it
will delete your history permanently. The app's Settings screen provides
a backup you can copy out and store wherever you choose.

Deleting your data
Settings includes "Delete all data", which erases the file from your
device immediately. Deleting the app removes it as well. Because we never
hold a copy, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for
us to delete on your behalf.

Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information
from anyone.

Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a
new date. Any change that involved collecting data would be announced in
the app before it took effect.
